How the system works

Structure, insulation and finish in one coordinated panel.

The panel uses corrosion-resistant metal skins around a high-performance insulated core. Interlocking joints, hidden fasteners and precision-formed profiles support a cleaner installation with reduced thermal bridging and fewer opportunities for water intrusion.

  • Wall, floor and roof applications
  • 100 mm (4 in.) option with R28.8 thermal performance
  • 150 mm (6 in.) higher-performance option
  • Factory-painted galvanized steel or aluminum skins
  • Heavy-gauge painted aluminum structural members
  • Integrated roof clips for solar mounting without unnecessary penetrations

Acrylic pool glass

Crystal-clear views.
Engineered to last.

Custom casting-acrylic panels transform pools and water features into premium architectural experiences while maintaining strength, clarity and long-term UV performance.

Premium material

Built around the exact pool geometry.

UNILUX acrylic pool panels can be produced in customizable shapes, sizes and thicknesses. The material uses high-quality casting PMMA manufactured from virgin Mitsubishi Chemical MMA monomer.

  • Custom panel dimensions and shapes
  • Excellent optical clarity and light transmission
  • UV resistance and protection against yellowing
  • Suitable for swimming pools, infinity edges and aquariums
  • Professional installation guidance, grout and sealant specification
  • Manufacturer-backed limited warranty options

Engineering & installation

Every opening is treated as an engineered detail.

01

Project review

Pool dimensions, water depth, visible area, panel support and embedment are reviewed.

02

Panel engineering

Thickness and panel geometry are selected for hydrostatic pressure, deflection and long-term load.

03

Rebate preparation

The concrete opening, support blocks and rebate geometry are coordinated before delivery.

04

Sealing support

Structural adhesive, grout and sealants are specified to accommodate movement and prevent leaks.
